TESTING PRO - NO KEY NEEDED
Pro is granted by the AppSource plan through IVisualLicenseManager; no activation key
exists. In EDIT mode without a licence, each Pro feature (collection styles, variance
label, small multiples, events, annotations, show values as) is drawn WORKING under a
"Pro preview" watermark that names it, with Power BI's licence notice. Intentional.
Reading view shows the free chart (small multiples: first panel and a note). Without
licence information (not signed in to Power BI Desktop): free chart, no watermark.

WHAT TO CHECK
- Empty visual: start screen with 3 steps; resize it.
- Click a point: other visuals filter; Ctrl+click adds; click again clears. Bookmark,
clear, apply: the selection comes back. Right-click: context menu.
- Keyboard: arrows move between points and series, Enter selects, Esc clears.
- High contrast: series differ by dash and marker shape.
- Format mode: click a line, an end label, an axis or the legend.

SETUP GUIDE AND LINKS
The setup guide's buttons call persistProperties on a user click only, never in the
selection callback. Start screen links (Guide, Video, Product page) open only on click
or Enter, through host.launchUrl.

in the visual says so.
