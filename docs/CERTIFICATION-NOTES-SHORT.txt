WHAT CHANGED IN 1.0.1.0
- Accessibility card (end of Format pane): High contrast Auto/On, focus outline color
and width, minimum text size.
- fetchMoreData when Power BI marks a segment, Desktop included, up to 10 more rounds;
one started/finished pair per update().
- Selecting a series (legend, line or end label) sends the identities of ALL its rows.
1.0.0.0 filtered a single row (one month) when the axis/legend made several columns.
- Over 1,000 rows, select() is not called: the selection is shown in the visual only,
with "Too many rows to filter other visuals (N) - select fewer items".
- Context menu on a series: no data point; on a point: its row.
- Per-series color/line style/width/markers kept by legend value (hidden object written
by one persistProperties per change while editing, never in selection callbacks).
Reports from 1.0.0.0 migrate once when opened in edit mode.

HOW TO TEST SERIES SELECTION (LineChart_sample.pbix, page 2, Legend = Region)
1. Add a Card with Sum of the same measure next to the chart.
2. Click a legend item, the line or its end label: the card shows the total of the whole
series, not one month. Ctrl+click adds a series; click the background to clear.
3. Click a point: the card shows that point only. Right-click a series and a point.
4. Small multiples (page 3): a click in a panel selects the series in that panel; the
legend selects it in every panel.
5. Limit: put a daily date on Axis with several years, then select several series until
the selection passes 1,000 rows: the notice appears and the card is not filtered.
